There are hundreds of VPNs, and most "best VPN" lists are affiliate pages. Here's a short,
honest checklist you can run in five minutes.

## 1. No‑logs, and ideally audited

A VPN that keeps connection logs can hand your activity to anyone who asks. Look for a clear
**no‑logs policy** — bonus points if it's been through an independent audit.

## 2. Modern protocol

You want **WireGuard** (fast, lean, modern crypto) with OpenVPN as a fallback. Anything still
pushing only PPTP or L2TP in 2026 is a red flag.

## 3. The basics that prevent leaks

- A **kill switch** that blocks traffic if the tunnel drops.
- **DNS leak protection** so your lookups don't bypass the tunnel.
- IPv6 handling (leak‑proof or disabled).

## 4. Jurisdiction and ownership

Who runs it, and where? Transparency about the operating company matters more than a flag on
a map.

## 5. Price that matches the value

Free VPNs have to make money somehow — often by selling the very data you're trying to protect.
Paid doesn't automatically mean private, but "free" deserves extra scrutiny.
