<h2 id="section-7">7. Common Mistakes People Make When Picking a Free iPad VPN</h2>

- **Confusing "Free App" with "Free Service"**: Many apps are free to download, but completely lock their interface until you start a paid trial.
- **Ignoring iPadOS battery drain**: Poorly optimized VPN apps running legacy OpenVPN protocols can quickly drain tablet batteries. Modern WireGuard tunneling drastically cuts CPU overhead.
- **Using sketchy browser extensions instead of system VPNs**: Browser-only extensions leave iPad background apps (mail, banking, messaging) completely unprotected.
