<p style="margin-bottom: 16px; line-height: 1.75; color: #09090b; font-size: 1.05rem;">OllaVPN offers essential security features like a default kill switch and in-tunnel DNS, along with advanced options like QUIC for restrictive networks, to protect your privacy.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 16px; line-height: 1.75; color: #09090b; font-size: 1.05rem;">You're probably looking for a VPN because you care about your privacy, and for good reason. Beyond just encrypting your connection, there are a few core features that really lock down your security. The first is a **kill switch**. This is crucial because even the most reliable VPN can sometimes momentarily drop its connection. Without a kill switch, your device might briefly revert to your regular, unprotected internet connection, potentially exposing your real IP address and unencrypted traffic. With OllaVPN, the kill switch is always on by default, so you never have to remember to enable it yourself. If the VPN connection ever drops, it immediately cuts your internet access, keeping you safe until the VPN reconnects. Another critical component is **DNS leak protection**. DNS (Domain Name System) is what translates human-readable website names (like "ollavpn.com") into computer-readable IP addresses. If your VPN isn't handling your DNS requests properly, your internet provider could still see which websites you're trying to visit, even if your traffic is encrypted. OllaVPN prevents this with **in-tunnel DNS**, meaning all your DNS requests are routed securely through the encrypted VPN tunnel, making sure your online activity stays private from end-to-end. While OllaVPN focuses on comprehensive protection for all your traffic, some VPNs offer **split tunneling**, which lets you choose which apps or websites use the VPN and which connect directly. We don't offer this feature because we believe that for true privacy, all your traffic should be protected by default. However, we do offer advanced tunneling options like QUIC, which can be particularly useful for bypassing restrictive network firewalls that might block traditional VPN protocols. This means you can stay connected and secure even in challenging network environments.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom: 16px; line-height: 1.75; color: #09090b; font-size: 1.05rem;">**Test 3, WebRTC and IPv6 leak.** Open <a href="/webrtc-leak-test" class="tldr-highlight-link" style="color: #DA291C; font-weight: 700; text-decoration: underline; text-underline-offset: 3px;">our WebRTC leak test</a>. <p style="margin-bottom: 16px; line-height: 1.75; color: #09090b; font-size: 1.05rem;">**Test 4, Kill switch.** Connect VPN, start a continuous ping in your terminal (`ping 1.1.1.1`), disable your network adapter.
